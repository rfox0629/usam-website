Be honest

How many times have you…

01…sat down with someone, landed on a real next step, and forgot to follow up?

02…said "I'm praying for you," with nowhere you actually keep those requests, and pray them?

03…watched God answer a prayer, and never paused to celebrate it?

04…started a reading plan or a book study with a few people, and watched it quietly die by week three?

05…wanted real accountability, but had no honest way to keep it going without someone chasing everyone down?

It's not that you don't care. You care deeply. But real ministry means dozens of people, hundreds of conversations, shared commitments, and countless things worth remembering, all held in your head, your notes app, and your best intentions.

Care at that scale needs a system.

More than reminders

A memory and an accountability partner, in one system.

DOS quietly holds every person, promise, prayer, and shared rhythm, and brings the right one back to you at the right time. You stay present with people. DOS keeps the record.

Remember

Every person, known.

The conversations you've had, the needs they've shared, the story you're part of. Kept, not scattered.

Follow through

Every promise, kept.

When you say "I'll follow up" or "I'm praying for you," DOS makes sure you actually do. Gently, on time.

Grow together

Every rhythm, alive.

Scripture sprints, book studies, shared commitments. Accountability that keeps going without anyone chasing anyone down.

Celebrate

Every answer, seen.

Look back and see what God has done: answered prayers, changed lives, and evidence of His faithfulness over time.

That's the shape of it. The rest is best experienced in a walkthrough, on purpose. We'd rather show you than publish the playbook.

Intelligent by design

It doesn't just remember. It discerns.

Anyone can store notes. DOS understands what it holds: it reads the whole picture of your ministry and turns it into discernment you can act on.

01

It surfaces the right person at the right time.

Not a feed of everything. The one conversation, prayer, or follow-up that matters most today, brought to you before it slips.

02

It notices what you'd miss.

Across months of ministry, patterns emerge: who's growing, who's quietly drifting, and what God keeps doing. DOS sees the long arc, so you can respond to it.

03

It connects your walk to your ministry.

You can't pour out what you're not receiving. DOS holds your own time with God alongside the people you care for, and keeps both honest.

Intelligence, surrendered.

All of that insight exists for one purpose: to help you respond faithfully to what God may be revealing. This is the posture the entire system is built on.

Pride says, "Here is my data."

Humility says, "Here is the data, Lord. What do You want me to do with it?"

Faithfulness over performance

01

Accountability without surveillance

Loving accountability that helps people follow through, never systems that make them feel watched. Privacy and dignity by design.

02

People, never numbers

Nothing in DOS reduces a person to a metric. Every view exists to help you care for someone better, not to score them.

03

Built for disciple-makers

For individuals, missionaries, churches, and ministry organizations who take intentional discipleship seriously.

Matthew 28:19-20

Built to multiply.

"Go therefore and make disciples of all nations." That command is the reason DOS exists: every person remembered, every promise kept, every disciple equipped to make disciples. DOS is not designed to help you become more productive. It is designed to help you faithfully fulfill the Great Commission.
