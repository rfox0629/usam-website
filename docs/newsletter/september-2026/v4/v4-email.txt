FIELD UPDATE // SEPTEMBER 2026
THERE'S A LOT WE'VE BEEN WANTING TO SHARE
Three parts of one mission: the people we are gathering and preparing, what disciple making actually looks like around a table, and the tool being built to help it keep going.
Q2 / Q3 2026
----------------------------------------
Hi Ryan,
There is a lot we have been wanting to share with you.

Over these past months the Lord has been bringing real clarity to how this ministry fits together. We have been meeting people, gathering around tables, walking with men in discipleship, and building a tool to help that work continue.

All of it sits under one mission: USA Missionaries exists to locate, train, and deploy disciple makers across America. Here is what that looks like right now, and where we believe the Lord is leading next.
----------------------------------------
01 / THE COVERING
THE MISSION IS TAKING SHAPE.
USA Missionaries is the home for all of this. It is where the people, the training, and the sending live.

As we have shared the vision, we have begun conversations with several couples who are exploring what it could look like to join us. We are listening, praying, and taking time to know each other well. Nothing is being announced yet, and we are not rushing it. We are simply encouraged by the people God keeps bringing into these conversations.

We also rebuilt the website so the mission explains itself: locating, training, and deploying disciple makers across America.
Explore USA Missionaries: https://usamissionaries.org
----------------------------------------
02 / THE MODEL IN ACTION
WHAT BEGINS AT A TABLE CAN KEEP GROWING.
Kitchen Table Gospel is the model, and it is not complicated. Real homes. Real tables. A meal, an open Bible, honest conversation, and prayer out loud.

What makes it discipleship is not the evening itself. It is what keeps going afterward.
----------------------------------------
MEN ARE GATHERING.
Two men's groups are also meeting consistently. They open Scripture together, pray, build real accountability, and keep walking through actual life with one another.

It is ordinary and it is working.
2three2 — Run. Pray. Pursue.
----------------------------------------
YOUR TABLE IS ENOUGH.
Invite someone in. Open Scripture together. Pray out loud, even if it is short. Then follow up during the week.

That is the whole model, and it is the one thing we would ask you to try.
Gather. Learn. Confess. Encourage. Multiply.
Explore Kitchen Table Gospel: https://kitchentablegospel.org
----------------------------------------
03 / THE TOOL BEING PREPARED
HELPING DISCIPLESHIP CONTINUE.
We are getting close to a broader launch of the Discipleship Operating System. It has been in limited use while we build it, shaped by the very work you have just read about.

It is made to help disciple makers carry the people, the prayers, and the next steps they promised, so discipleship keeps going between the gatherings.

Technology cannot replace a conversation, a prayer, or a relationship. We only hope it makes it easier to keep showing up for someone, and to help them do the same for someone else.
Request Information: https://discipleshipoperatingsystem.com
----------------------------------------
WE BELIEVE THIS IS ONLY THE BEGINNING.
Three parts, one mission. Gather and prepare people, show what disciple making actually looks like, and help it keep multiplying. That is all USA Missionaries is trying to do.

Thank you for praying for us, for encouraging us, and for partnering with this work. Would you pray for the couples we are talking with, for the people around these tables, for the two men's groups, and for the preparation ahead of a broader DOS launch?
Ryan & Brooke
