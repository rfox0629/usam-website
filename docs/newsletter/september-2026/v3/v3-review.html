Hi Ryan,

It has been a little while since our last update, and it has not been quiet.

Over the last several months, Brooke and I have watched God open doors, bring people into our lives, and make it much clearer what USA Missionaries is becoming.

Almost none of it happened on a stage. Most of it happened at a table.
 

From the Table

It happens
at a table.

This is what disciple making looks like on the ground. An ordinary home, an ordinary evening, a few chairs pulled in close. We eat, we open the Scriptures, we pray out loud, and we tell the truth about our lives.

And then the night ends, and the quieter work starts.

Kitchen Table Gospel // Minnesota

The gathering, and what came after

The one evening this issue is built around, and how we kept caring for her in the weeks that followed. Held until the source reflection is in hand and its Planning Center sharing permission is verified. No name, photograph, or disclosure goes here before then.
 
 

Your table is enough.

You do not need a building, a budget, or a title. You need a table and one person.

Invite someone in. Open the Scriptures together, even a few verses. Pray out loud, even if it is short and awkward. Then follow up during the week — a text, a call, a chair saved for next month.

That is the whole model, and it is the one thing we would ask you to try.

Gather. Learn. Confess. Encourage. Multiply.
