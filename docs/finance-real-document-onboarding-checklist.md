# Finance Real Document Onboarding Checklist

For Ryan, after the synthetic end-to-end test passes and synthetic records
are removed. This is a **checklist of what to gather and upload** — it does
not infer or assume any legal or accounting fact about USA Missionaries.
Every "Unknown" in the app (filing type, year-end, extension status, EIN,
etc.) stays Unknown until you confirm it here or in the app directly.

Upload real documents at `/ncc/finance` (general corporate/financial
documents) or on the specific filing workspace (AZ Annual Report / Form 990)
for filing-specific documents. Check items off as you go — nothing here
is required before you start using the app; it just tells the AI-assisted
suggestions and the filing workspaces what's still missing.

## Corporate / governance documents

- [ ] Arizona Corporation Commission approval of formation
- [ ] Articles of Incorporation (as filed and any amendments)
- [ ] IRS EIN confirmation letter (CP 575 or 147C)
- [ ] IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter
- [ ] Bylaws (current version, and prior version if amended)
- [ ] Current board of directors roster (names, roles, terms)
- [ ] Current officer roster (President, Treasurer, Secretary, etc.)
- [ ] Board meeting minutes for the filing year(s) in question
- [ ] Housing allowance designation/approval (board resolution)
- [ ] Conflict-of-interest policy (signed, current version)
- [ ] Certificate(s) of insurance (D&O, general liability, etc.)
- [ ] Any other state filings on record (charitable solicitation
      registration, foreign-entity registrations, etc.)

## Financial documents — banking

- [ ] Every bank statement for the filing year (all accounts, all months)
- [ ] Every credit card statement for the filing year (all cards, all
      months)
- [ ] Beginning-of-year balance for each account
- [ ] End-of-year balance for each account

## Financial documents — payroll

- [ ] Gusto (or other payroll provider) payroll summary reports for the
      filing year
- [ ] W-2s and W-3 for the filing year
- [ ] Payroll tax filings (941s, state unemployment, etc.) for the filing
      year

## Financial documents — giving and donors

- [ ] Donation/contribution reports for the filing year (by donor, by
      fund/restriction if tracked)
- [ ] Records of any restricted contributions and their restriction terms

## Financial documents — assets, liabilities, other

- [ ] List of equipment/assets owned (with approximate value/date acquired)
- [ ] List of outstanding liabilities (loans, notes payable, etc.)
- [ ] Loan agreements or promissory notes, if any
- [ ] Contractor records (1099s issued, contracts) for the filing year
- [ ] Reimbursement records/policy (mileage, expense reimbursement) for the
      filing year

## What this unblocks

- **Arizona Annual Report** — needs the corporate/governance documents plus
  current officer roster.
- **Form 990** — needs everything above; the 990 workspace will show
  specific missing-document flags per section (governance, program
  accomplishments, officer compensation, financial statements) as documents
  come in.

Nothing here files anything automatically. Uploading a document only makes
it available for the human-approved workpaper/package workflow — it never
changes a filing's status on its own.
