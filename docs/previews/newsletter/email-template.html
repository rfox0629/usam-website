Dark-mode behavior is controlled by the reader's mail client, not a browser toggle — most browsers won't simulate prefers-color-scheme on an iframe without a devtools override. To see the dark variant, open email-template-source.html directly and toggle your OS/browser to dark mode (devtools → Rendering → "Emulate CSS prefers-color-scheme: dark" in Chrome/Edge), or read the dark-mode rules directly in that file's <style> block.
