Updates from the field

This month brought a breakthrough we've been praying toward for over a year, a new discipleship group taking root, and a simple way you can keep standing with us. Thank you for walking this road with our family.

A kitchen table, three new believers

What began as a weekly coffee conversation turned into a full discipleship table this month. Three friends who had never opened a Bible together are now meeting weekly, asking hard questions, and starting to trust Jesus with their lives.

What's next

We're starting a second table next month with two more families. Pray for open hearts, and for wisdom as we disciple people who are brand new to faith.
